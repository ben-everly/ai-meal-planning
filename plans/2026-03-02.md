# Meal Plan: Week of 2026-03-02

## Sunday Prep (2-hour session)

- [ ] Cook Quinoa: Make a large batch (2 cups dry) to use Monday and Tuesday.
- [ ] Roast Chicken: Bake or pressure-cook 1.5 lbs chicken breasts; shred for Monday and Tuesday.
- [ ] Roast Vegetables: Chop and roast 2 heads broccoli, 3 carrots, 1 onion, and 1 bell pepper.
- [ ] Make Tahini Dressing: Whisk tahini, lemon juice, olive oil, and warm water.
- [ ] Boil Eggs: Hard-boil 4 eggs for quick breakfasts.
- [ ] Prep Veggies: Chop cucumbers for snacks.

## Weekly Highlights

- **Snack 1:** Hummus with Cucumber Sticks
- **Snack 2:** Apples with Walnut Butter
- **Snack 3:** Low-Sodium Mozzarella Cheese Sticks
- **Dessert:** Fudgy Chocolate Peanut Butter Brownie Bites
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 2 Ripe bananas, mashed
    - [ ] 1/2 cup Peanut butter
    - [ ] 1/4 cup Cocoa powder
    - [ ] 1/2 cup Rolled oats (blended into flour)
  - **Preparation:** 1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Blend oats into a fine flour. 2. Mix oat flour, mashed bananas, peanut butter, and cocoa powder until a thick batter forms. 3. Spoon into a greased mini muffin tin or bake in an 8x8 pan. 4. Bake for 15-20 minutes until set. Enjoy warm!

## Monday

- **Breakfast:** Peanut Butter Oatmeal
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 1/2 cup Rolled oats
    - [ ] 1 cup Water
    - [ ] 1 tbsp Peanut butter
  - **Preparation:** 1. Microwave or boil oats with water until cooked (3-5 mins). 2. Stir in peanut butter and cool for baby.
- **Lunch:** Chicken & Veggie Quinoa Bowl
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 1 cup Cooked Quinoa (from prep)
    - [ ] 1/2 cup Shredded Chicken (from prep)
    - [ ] 1 cup Roasted Vegetables (from prep)
    - [ ] 2 tbsp Tahini Dressing (from prep)
  - **Preparation:** 1. Warm quinoa, chicken, and veggies. 2. Drizzle with tahini dressing.
- **Dinner:** 15-Minute Chicken & Veggie Noodle Soup
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 4 cups Low-sodium chicken broth
    - [ ] 1 cup Whole wheat noodles or macaroni
    - [ ] 1 cup Shredded Chicken (from prep)
    - [ ] 1 cup Roasted Vegetables (from prep), chopped
  - **Preparation:**
        1. Bring the chicken broth to a boil in a medium pot.
        2. Add the noodles and cook according to package instructions (about 8-10 mins).
        3. Stir in the shredded chicken and chopped roasted veggies. Simmer for 2-3 minutes until everything is heated through.
        4. Serve warm (ensure noodles and veggies are soft and cut safely for baby).

## Wednesday

- **Breakfast:** Scrambled Eggs and Whole Wheat Toast
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 2 Eggs
    - [ ] 1 slice Whole wheat bread
    - [ ] 1 tsp Unsalted butter
  - **Preparation:** 1. Scramble eggs in butter until cooked through. 2. Toast bread and cut into strips for baby.
- **Lunch:** (Leftovers from Tuesday Dinner)
- **Dinner:** 15-Minute Lemon Garlic Shrimp & Zucchini Noodles
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 1/2 lb Medium shrimp, peeled and deveined
    - [ ] 2 Zucchinis, chopped into chunks
    - [ ] 1 cup Whole wheat noodles
    - [ ] 1 tbsp Olive oil
    - [ ] 1 clove Garlic, minced
    - [ ] 1/2 Lemon, juiced
  - **Preparation:**
        1. Boil whole wheat noodles according to package instructions.
        2. While noodles cook, heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
        3. Add shrimp and zucchini chunks, cooking until shrimp is pink and zucchini is tender-crisp (about 3-4 mins).
        4. Toss the cooked noodles, shrimp, and zucchini with lemon juice, cooking for 1-2 mins until just warm. Serve immediately.
  - _Note:_ Total time is under 15 minutes. Cut shrimp into small pieces for baby.

## Friday

- **Breakfast:** (Eat Out)
- **Lunch:** (Eat Out)
- **Dinner:** Baked Salmon with Orzo and Asparagus
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 1 lb Salmon fillet
    - [ ] 1 bunch Asparagus, trimmed
    - [ ] 1/2 cup Orzo
    - [ ] 1 tbsp Olive oil
    - [ ] 1/2 Lemon, juiced
  - **Preparation:**
        1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Place salmon and asparagus on a baking sheet, drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice. Bake for 12-15 minutes.
        2. Meanwhile, boil orzo according to package instructions (about 9-10 minutes), then drain.
        3. Flake salmon carefully, ensuring no bones for baby. Serve alongside asparagus and orzo.

## Saturday

- **Breakfast:** Hard-Boiled Egg & Apple
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 1 Hard-boiled egg (from prep)
    - [ ] 1/2 Apple, thinly sliced/steamed for baby
    - [ ] 1 tbsp Walnut butter
  - **Preparation:** 1. Peel and slice the egg. 2. Serve with apple slices and walnut butter for dipping.
- **Lunch:** (Leftovers from Friday Dinner)
- **Dinner:** Turkey Meatballs with Broccoli and Bell Peppers
  - **Ingredients:**
    - [ ] 1/2 lb Ground turkey
    - [ ] 1/4 cup Whole wheat breadcrumbs
    - [ ] 1 Egg
    - [ ] 1 Bell pepper, sliced
    - [ ] 1 cup Broccoli florets
    - [ ] 1 tbsp Olive oil
    - [ ] 1/2 tsp Garlic powder
    - [ ] 1/2 tsp Onion powder
  - **Preparation:**
        1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
        2. Mix turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, garlic powder, and onion powder. Form small meatballs.
        3. Place meatballs on a baking sheet. Bake for 10 minutes.
        4. Toss broccoli and bell peppers with olive oil. Add to the baking sheet with the meatballs.
        5. Bake for another 10-12 minutes until meatballs are cooked through and veggies are tender.
